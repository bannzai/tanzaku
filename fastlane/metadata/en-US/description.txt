Tanzaku is a snippet manager for iPhone. Save boilerplate text, commands, code and AI prompts, and pull them up the moment you need them.

- Find snippets even when you forget the abbreviation
As your snippets grow, you stop remembering their keywords. Along with fuzzy search over titles, bodies and keywords, Tanzaku uses semantic search, including Japanese, to find a snippet from whatever fragment you remember. Searching happens on your iPhone.

- Type snippets straight from the keyboard
Switch to the Tanzaku keyboard to pick a snippet and type it into the text field of any app. The keyboard neither stores nor sends what you type.

- Save from the share sheet
Save text or a URL you selected in another app as a snippet from the share sheet.

- Call them up from search and Shortcuts
Find snippets by title and keyword from iPhone search. Search for a snippet and copy it from Shortcuts or the Action button.

- Sync with Mac
Your snippets sync with Tanzaku for Mac through iCloud.

- No account
Snippets are stored on your iPhone and in your iCloud, and never sent to the developer's servers.

- One-time purchase
Try every feature for free, up to 20 snippets. A single purchase removes the limit.

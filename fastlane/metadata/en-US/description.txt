Tanzaku is a snippet manager for Mac. Save boilerplate text, commands, code and AI prompts, and pull them up from a launcher you open with a shortcut.

- Find snippets even when you forget the abbreviation
As your snippets grow, you stop remembering their keywords. Along with fuzzy search over titles, bodies and keywords, the launcher uses semantic search, including Japanese, to find a snippet from whatever fragment you remember. Searching happens on your Mac.

- Copy, or paste right away
The snippet you pick is copied to the clipboard. Grant the Accessibility permission and Tanzaku pastes it into the front app for you.

- Let AI agents organize them
Tanzaku has a built-in MCP server. AI agents that support MCP can search, add, update and delete snippets. The server accepts connections only from the same Mac, and only from clients holding a token Tanzaku issued.

- Deletions need Touch ID
When an AI agent tries to delete a snippet, Tanzaku asks for Touch ID. You can hand the work over and still decide what gets deleted.

- Your snippets stay on your Mac
No account. Snippets are stored only on your Mac and never sent to the developer's servers.

- One-time purchase
Try every feature for free, up to 20 snippets. A single purchase removes the limit.

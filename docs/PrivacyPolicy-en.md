# Privacy Policy

bannzai ("we") sets out this Privacy Policy for the handling of user information, including personal information, in the macOS app "Tanzaku" (the "Service").

## Information we handle and how

### Information you enter (stored only on your Mac)

The snippets you save in the Service (titles, bodies, keywords and tags) and your settings are **stored only on your Mac, inside the Service's sandbox container, and are never sent to our servers**. We cannot collect or view them. Searches, including semantic search, run on your Mac unless you turn on the optional external semantic search described below.

- You are responsible for backing up your snippets. We cannot recover snippets lost by replacing or resetting your Mac or by deleting the Service.

### AI agent integration (MCP)

So that AI agents you connect (such as Claude Code) can search, add, update and delete snippets, the Service runs a Model Context Protocol (MCP) server on your Mac. The server accepts connections only from the same Mac (127.0.0.1) and only from clients holding a token issued by the Service.

Snippets you hand to a connected AI agent are handled under the terms and privacy policy of that agent's provider. You choose which AI agents to connect and can revoke a connection in the Service's settings.

### Optional external semantic search (sending to an external AI service; off by default)

Only if you turn on TypeSafe's Jev in the settings and enter your own API key, the Service sends your search query and the bodies of the snippets being searched to the API of TypeSafe, Inc. ( https://typesafe.ai/ ) when you search in the launcher. Nothing is sent unless you agree to the explanation shown before turning it on, and you can turn it off at any time. What is sent is handled under the terms and privacy policy of TypeSafe, Inc. We do not receive it.

### Information collected by external services

To manage the purchase status of the paid license, the Service uses RevenueCat, provided by RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat collects purchase information (the product purchased, the purchase date and time, an anonymous device identifier, and so on). It does not include information that identifies you, such as your name or email address, nor the contents of your snippets. It is handled under RevenueCat, Inc.'s privacy policy ( https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy ).

In-app purchases are processed by Apple Inc. We do not receive payment information such as credit card details.

### Information we collect

The Service requires no account, and we do not collect or store your personal information on servers. The Service contains no advertising or analytics SDK. Only when you contact us by email or otherwise do we use your contact details and inquiry to respond.

## Purposes of use

- To provide, maintain, protect and improve the Service, including checking and restoring the purchase status of the paid license
- To send notices about the Service and to respond to inquiries
- To respond to conduct that violates our terms or policies for the Service
- To notify you of changes to the terms and policies for the Service

## Retention

- Snippets and settings stay on your Mac until you delete them in the Service or delete the Service
- Purchase information is retained for the period set out in RevenueCat, Inc.'s privacy policy
- Contact details and inquiries are kept as long as we judge necessary as a record of our response, and are then deleted

## Provision to third parties

We do not provide personal information to third parties (including those outside Japan) without your prior consent, except in the following cases:

- When we entrust all or part of the handling of personal information within the scope necessary to achieve the purposes of use (processor: RevenueCat, Inc. (United States))
- When personal information is provided as part of a business succession due to a merger or other reason
- When cooperation is required with a national or local government body, or a party entrusted by one, in carrying out duties prescribed by law, and obtaining your consent could impede those duties
- Other cases permitted by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information or other laws

## Disclosure, correction, suspension of use and deletion

When you request disclosure, correction, suspension of use or deletion of your personal information under the Act on the Protection of Personal Information, we will respond without delay after confirming that the request comes from you (and will notify you if no such personal information exists), except where we have no obligation under that Act or other laws.

We do not hold snippets and other data stored only on your Mac, so you can delete them yourself:

1. Delete snippets in the Service's management window
2. To delete all data, quit the Service, remove it from the Applications folder, and delete `~/Library/Containers/com.bannzai.tanzaku`
